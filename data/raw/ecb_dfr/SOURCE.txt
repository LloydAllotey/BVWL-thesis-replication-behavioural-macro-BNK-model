ecb_key_interest_rates.csv

Source: European Central Bank, "Key ECB interest rates",
https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/key_ecb_interest_rates/html/index.en.html
Table as shown on the page, retrieved 8 November 2026. Interest rate levels in percentages per
annum, one row per change, with the date "with effect from". The footnote markers behind some dates
were dropped, the minus sign written as "-", and empty cells ("-" on the page) left empty.
An ISO date (date) was added in front of the page's own columns.

Columns: date, year, date_with_effect_from, deposit_facility, main_refinancing_fixed_rate_tenders,
main_refinancing_variable_rate_tenders_minimum_bid_rate, marginal_lending_facility.

Note from the page: from 10 March 2004 onwards the date refers to the standing facilities (deposit
and marginal lending) as well as to the main refinancing operations. As of 9 October 2008 the
corridor of the standing facilities was reduced from 200 to 100 basis points. Effective
18 September 2024 the spread between the main refinancing rate and the deposit facility rate was
reduced to 15 basis points.
